Lipoedema

A simple diagnosis

For someone who knows the condition, lipoedema is extremely simple to diagnose clinically, without sophisticated additional tests. With two or three well-chosen explanations, you can recognise it yourself.

Lipoedema pain
The chronic pain of lipoedema is caused by the fat compressing the deep vessels and peripheral micro-vessels, which prevents the tissues from being properly oxygenated.
Treatments
According to my patients' feedback, the compression stockings recommended by many worsen the pain rather than relieve it. And that makes sense: compressing fat has never made anyone lose a single gram of it. The only way to help you is to remove this fat which, once formed, cannot be eliminated through a balanced diet and exercise, although both are still recommended to keep the condition from getting worse.
