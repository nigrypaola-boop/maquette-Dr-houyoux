Liposculpture

Liposculpture with Lipomatic / Tickle Lipo

My technique of choice since 1997

From my very first training courses, I was passionate about liposculpture and decided to perfect my skills in this field by studying, alongside various colleagues, the techniques I found most promising.

Vibroliposculpture with the Lipomatic, combined with Klein's tumescent technique, offers a far more comprehensive approach to the lipodystrophy problems that alter our patients' silhouettes. I have been using vibroliposculpture, also known as Nutational Infrasonic Liposculpture, since 1997. It remains my technique of choice. It was introduced in the United States in 2010 under the name Tickle Lipo.

Mastery of liposculpture reduces bruising and the discomfort of the post-operative period. It allows true sculpting of areas known to be difficult, such as the lower buttocks, or even off-limits with conventional techniques, such as the central part of the buttocks.

It improves skin retraction, even when the skin has lost its natural elasticity, as after menopause. While liposculpture is not a treatment for obesity without a diet, combined with one it can help restore a silhouette that improves our patients' well-being.

These results were obtained solely with the “Houyoux cannula” (2 mm in diameter).

Thanks to these new techniques, the indications for liposculpture have been broadened and the expected results improved in areas such as the jawline.

What makes my method unique

A cannula developed by Dr Houyoux

At the end of 2014, after years of research, I developed a cannula that allows me to work with extreme precision in the last few millimetres of the fat layer.

2 mm

A fine cannula

2 mm in diameter, with six 1 mm holes, driven by the movement of the Lipomatic.
